Emergency Guides

CPR

1. Call emergency services.
2. Place hands in center of chest.
3. Push hard and fast.
4. 100–120 compressions per minute.

Choking

1. Ask if they can cough.
2. Give 5 back blows.
3. Perform abdominal thrusts.
4. Call emergency services.

Bleeding

1. Apply firm pressure.
2. Use clean cloth or bandage.
3. Elevate injury if possible.
4. Seek medical help.
