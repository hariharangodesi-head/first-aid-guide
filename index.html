Choking

1. Ask if they can cough.
2. Give 5 back blows.
3. Perform abdominal thrusts.
4. Call emergency services.

Bleeding

1. Apply firm pressure.
2. Use clean cloth.
3. Elevate injury.
4. Seek help.

Burns

1. Cool burn under running water.
2. Cover with sterile gauze.
3. Do not apply ointments.
4. Seek medical help.

Fractures

1. Immobilize area.
2. Apply ice.
3. Keep limb elevated.
4. Seek medical attention.

Shock

1. Lay the person down.
2. Raise legs if possible.
3. Keep warm.
4. Call emergency services immediately.
